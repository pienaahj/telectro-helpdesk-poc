# Emerald Life Customer Welcome Guide

## Main purpose

The Customer portal gives Emerald Life users a simple way to:

- log support requests for Emerald Life sites;
- identify the affected service area and severity;
- choose the affected Emerald Life Campus;
- provide an equipment, circuit, SIM, tag, or other reference when one is known;
- attach photos or other useful evidence;
- view progress;
- add more information when needed.

Emerald Life reports Customer-facing faults at **Campus level**.

The affected Campus is therefore the Customer-facing fault location. Detailed equipment or network investigation is handled by the Telectro team where required.

The Customer portal is not a formal sign-off system. Telectro confirms completion through the normal service process and then resolves the request from the Telectro side.

---

## Customer portal

### Access

Customer portal:

`https://erp.telectro.co.za/helpdesk/my-tickets`

If you are not already signed in, you will be taken to the login page first.

Customer users should use:

`Support Requests`

The Emerald Life portal is presented as the **Emerald Life Service Desk** and is managed by Telectro.

### First-time access

When your Customer account is created, you receive a system-generated welcome email.

Use the link in that email to:

1. open the Telectro ERPNext password setup page;
2. choose your password; and
3. complete your first login.

The setup link is time-limited. If it is no longer valid, request a new password setup or password-reset email rather than reusing an old link.

After successful setup, the original setup link can no longer be used.

## Basic Customer workflow

1. Log in with your own Customer account.
2. Open `Support Requests`.
3. Check whether the same issue is already logged.
4. Select `Log a Support Request` when this is a new issue.
5. Choose the relevant `Service Area`.
6. Choose the appropriate `Severity`.
7. Choose the affected Emerald Life `Campus`.
8. Add an equipment, circuit, SIM, tag, ICCID, or other useful reference if one is known.
9. Add a short, clear `Subject`.
10. Describe the issue and any useful location or access information.
11. Attach photos, labels, screenshots, or other evidence where helpful.
12. Submit the request.
13. Return to the request later to view progress or use `Add information`.

If the issue is already logged, open the existing request and use `Add information` instead of creating a duplicate request.

## Support Requests

The `Support Requests` page is the normal starting point for Customer portal work.

Use it to:

- check whether an issue is already logged;
- view existing support requests available to your account;
- see the current status;
- return to an existing request;
- read Customer-visible updates; and
- add more information when needed.

Select `Log a Support Request` when a new issue needs to be reported.

## Log a Support Request

The new-request page asks for the information Telectro needs to identify and route the issue.

The main fields are:

- `Service Area` — required;
- `Severity` — required;
- `Equipment / Circuit / SIM / Tag reference` — optional when a useful reference is known;
- `Campus` — the affected Emerald Life site;
- `Subject` — required; and
- the request description.

A good request explains:

- what is not working;
- which Campus is affected;
- what the Customer is seeing or experiencing;
- when the issue was noticed, where useful;
- any known equipment or circuit reference; and
- any access or contact information Telectro may need.

## Choosing the affected Campus

Emerald Life reports Customer-facing faults at **Campus level**.

The Campus selector is limited to locations belonging to the Emerald Life Customer organisation.

The current Emerald Life Customer scope contains **24 Campuses**:

- Head Office; and
- 23 active branch Campuses.

Choose the Campus where the issue is occurring.

For example:

`Emerald Life - Head Office`

The Customer does not need to choose a building, room, network node, handset, router, or other deeper location below the Campus.

Once an Emerald Life Campus is selected, the portal treats that Campus as the selected fault location.

## Equipment, circuit, SIM, or tag references

The field:

`Equipment / Circuit / SIM / Tag reference`

can be used when the Customer already knows a useful identifying reference.

Examples may include:

- an equipment serial or label;
- a circuit reference;
- a SIM or ICCID reference; or
- another identifier already known to the Customer.

This field does **not** replace the Campus selection.

If no useful reference is known, leave it blank and describe the issue clearly.

Telectro can investigate the detailed equipment or logical network path after the request has been received.

---

## Subject and description

Use a short subject that makes the issue easy to recognise.

For example:

`PABX issue at Head Office`

The description should give Telectro enough information to understand the problem without requiring the Customer to diagnose the underlying technical cause.

Useful information may include:

- what is not working;
- whether the issue affects one user or a wider site;
- what the Customer has already observed;
- any relevant error message;
- access information; and
- any known equipment or service reference.

## After submitting a request

After submission, the portal opens the support-request detail page.

The page shows Customer-visible information such as:

- the request number;
- status;
- Customer-visible activity;
- the selected fault location;
- subject;
- service area;
- severity; and
- response and resolution timing where applicable.

For Emerald Life, the selected Campus appears as the request's `Fault Point`.

Deeper `Category` and `Affected Equipment` details may remain blank because Emerald Life currently reports faults at Campus level.

> Screenshot: Emerald Life support-request detail page showing the selected Campus and request details.

## Latest update and Activity

When Customer-visible information is added to a request, the portal shows it in the request activity.

The most recent Customer-visible message is also shown prominently as the `Latest update`.

Use the activity history to understand what information has already been provided and what has changed since the request was logged.

Internal Telectro notes, private coordination, and information not intended for Customer visibility are not part of the Customer-facing activity stream.

> Screenshot: Emerald Life support request showing Latest update and the Customer-visible Activity history.

## Add information

Use `Add information` when an existing request needs more detail.

Examples include:

- a new observation;
- a corrected or more exact location description;
- an equipment label or reference;
- an access note;
- a screenshot;
- a photograph; or
- another relevant update.

Do not create a second request merely to add information to an issue that is already logged.

Select `Add information`, enter the new information, attach evidence where useful, and select `Send`.

The update then appears in the Customer-visible Activity history.

> Screenshot: Add information editor for an Emerald Life support request.

---

## Photos, files, and evidence

Attach evidence when it helps Telectro understand or investigate the issue.

Useful evidence may include:

- photographs;
- screenshots;
- equipment labels;
- error messages;
- circuit or SIM information; and
- access or location notes.

Only include information relevant to the support request.

Customers should not assume that every file or attachment held internally by Telectro will appear in the Customer portal.

## What Customer users should do

- Use your own Customer login.
- Check `Support Requests` before logging a duplicate issue.
- Choose the affected Emerald Life Campus.
- Give the request a short, clear subject.
- Explain what is wrong and where.
- Add a useful equipment or service reference when one is known.
- Add photos or evidence when they help.
- Return to the existing request when more information becomes available.
- Use `Add information` for follow-up details.

## What Customer users should not do

- Do not create duplicate requests for the same issue.
- Do not guess at a deeper technical fault location if it is not known.
- Do not invent equipment, circuit, SIM, or tag references.
- Do not create artificial building or equipment detail below an Emerald Life Campus.
- Do not use the Customer portal as a formal approval or sign-off process.
- Do not expect internal Telectro notes or coordination to appear in the Customer portal.

---

## When the Customer action is complete

Customer portal work is complete when the Customer has done what is needed from their side, for example:

- logged a new support request;
- supplied the affected Campus;
- supplied useful evidence or references;
- responded with additional information; or
- reviewed the Customer-visible progress on the request.

Telectro remains responsible for operational ownership, investigation, coordination, and resolution.

Formal Customer portal sign-off is not required before Telectro resolves or closes the support request.
